# Evidence and safety

## Warning signs come first

Every "I'm feeling…" page starts with warning signs taken from NHS condition pages, sorted into three levels:

| Level | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Emergency** | The page tells you to call your local emergency number. **No foods are shown.** |
| **See a doctor today** | The page says so, and no foods are shown. |
| **Book a doctor** | The advice comes first, with the foods below it. |

## Three evidence grades

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Strong** | A guideline, position stand or WHO statement backs this use of the food or its nutrient. |
| **Some** | At least one trial or review supports it. Often the trial used a supplement or extract, and the page says so. |
| **Traditional** | Common traditional use with no good trial behind it. Listed last, and labelled. |

Each food on a feeling page links the study or guideline behind its grade.

## What Eat This never does

It never diagnoses, treats or promises an outcome. It never suggests a dose, a supplement or a medicine. If something
feels seriously wrong, it's not a food question: call your local emergency number.
