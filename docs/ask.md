# Ask and Speak

[Ask](https://eatthis.aoneahsan.com/ask) answers a food question in your own words: *"Cheap protein for a vegetarian?"*
or *"What should I eat when I'm tired in the afternoon?"*

- Answers name up to five foods from the Eat This list, each with one line on what it gives. Tap a food for its numbers.
- Your diet and the foods you leave out shape the answer.
- No doses, no medicines, no diagnoses. A sudden or severe symptom gets "talk to a doctor" and no foods.
- Questions a day: 3 as a guest, 10 signed in, 50 on Plus, 60 each on Family. The count resets every morning.

## Speak

Signed in, press **Speak**, say your question (up to a minute) and press **Stop**. The words land in the box so you can
check them before you press **Ask**. The recording is kept only while it's turned into words, then deleted.
