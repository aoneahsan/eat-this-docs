# Reminders

A reminder is a nudge at a time you choose, naming one food that fits how you eat and the number behind it. It comes
from your phone, in the Eat This app for Android, so it works offline. Open **Reminders** in the app to add a time,
turn one off or remove it.

## What a reminder says

Each reminder carries the name of its part of the day as the title and one line under it:
*food: amount nutrient in serving.* With no filters set, a breakfast reminder reads:

> **Breakfast**
> Soybeans (cooked): 31 g protein in 1 cup cooked.

The food is the top pick for that part of the day on [Now](./ways-in.md#now), worked out on your phone from your own
[filters](./filters.md): your diet, the foods you leave out, and the cheap band if you chose to keep it cheap. Change a
filter and the next reminder follows it. When nothing on the list fits every filter for that time, the reminder says
so instead of guessing: *"Lunch: open Eat This for a pick that fits your diet."*

## Which nutrient each time leads with

When you add a time, Eat This files it under the part of the day the time falls in. That part decides the nutrient.

| Part of the day | From | To | Leads with |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early morning | 04:00 | 07:00 | fibre |
| Breakfast | 07:00 | 10:00 | protein |
| Mid-morning | 10:00 | 12:00 | vitamin C |
| Lunch | 12:00 | 15:00 | iron |
| Afternoon | 15:00 | 18:00 | protein |
| Evening | 18:00 | 21:00 | protein |
| Late night | 21:00 | 04:00 | calcium |

These are the same parts of the day the [daily plan](./daily-plan.md) fills, one meal each.

## How many times you can set

| | Free | Plus | Family |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reminder times | 3 | 8 | 8 each |

A new install starts with two, both on: 08:00 (breakfast) and 16:00 (afternoon). Once the list is full, the page says
so; remove one to add another. See [Plans and privacy](./plans-and-privacy.md) for what else each plan includes.

## Quiet hours

Nothing arrives during your quiet hours. They run from 22:00 to 07:00 until you change them in **Settings**. A reminder
time inside them stays in your list, marked *inside quiet hours, so it stays silent*, and starts again if you move
the quiet hours.

## On Android, in a browser, and across devices

- **Android:** the app asks once for permission to show notifications. If you say no, the Reminders page tells you,
  and the reminders start as soon as you allow notifications for Eat This in your phone's settings.
- **In a browser:** reminders don't arrive. Your times are kept for when you install the app.
- **Signed in:** your reminder times are saved to your account, so a new phone starts with the same list. On Family,
  each person keeps their own (see [Your account](./account.md)).

## Why one can arrive a few minutes late

Eat This never asks for the exact-alarm permission. Google Play keeps that permission for alarm clocks and calendars,
and a food nudge doesn't need to land to the second. Without it, Android may hold a reminder back a little to save
battery, so it can arrive a few minutes after the time you set. That's on purpose.

## Reminders, news and your calendar

- **News from Eat This** is a separate switch at the bottom of the Reminders page: a note when a food, a feeling or a
  correction to a number is added, a few a month at most. Reminders work without it.
- **Calendar:** your reminder times can also show as daily events in Google Calendar or Apple Calendar, through a
  private address. See
  [Calendar feed](./developers/calendar-feed.md).
- **Other apps:** an access key with the reminder scopes can list, add, move or remove times. See
  [Access keys](./developers/access-keys.md).

Deleting your account removes your reminder times with everything else. A reminder is a suggestion, not a diagnosis.
Check with your doctor, especially if you're pregnant, on medication or managing a condition.
