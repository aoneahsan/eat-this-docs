# Daily plan

Plus and Family include a [daily plan](https://eatthis.aoneahsan.com/plan): five foods for today, one each for
breakfast, mid-morning, lunch, the afternoon and the evening.

- Each meal takes the food that gives the most of what that time of day leads with (protein at breakfast, vitamin C
  mid-morning, iron at lunch), within your diet, the foods you leave out and your budget.
- No food appears twice in a day, and the plan changes every day.
- **Swap** moves one meal to its next pick.
- Under the meals, the day's protein, fibre, iron, calcium and vitamin C are added up against a day's need.

On Free you can see today's plan as a preview.
