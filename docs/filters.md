# Filters

Every list and search has the same filter bar.

- **Diet:** everything, vegetarian (plants and dairy), eggetarian (plus eggs), vegan (plants only), pescatarian (no meat).
- **Budget:** any price, cheap only, or up to moderate.
- **Preparation:** ready to eat, needs cooking, soak first, grind first.
- **Leave out:** dairy, eggs, gluten, tree nuts, peanuts, soy, sesame, fish, shellfish.
- **Sort:** most per serving, most for the money, fewest calories, most per 100 g.

Filters live in the page address, so a filtered list can be bookmarked or shared.

## When a list runs thin

Some filters leave few real sources, and we say so rather than padding the list. The clearest case is **vitamin B12 for
vegans: no natural plant food on our list gives a meaningful amount.** The page tells you that, and suggests asking your
doctor about it.

Halal only or no pork? Nothing on our list is pork, and how meat is slaughtered is up to your butcher, so neither filter
would hide a food.
