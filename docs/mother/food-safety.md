# What to avoid or limit

## How the app uses this

While a stage is on, foods marked **avoid** are left out of every list for that stage. Every other food here stays in
the lists and carries a label with its rule.

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Avoid | Not advised at this stage; hidden from lists |
| Limit | Fine within the stated limit |
| Cook well | Fine when cooked as stated |
| Pasteurised only | Fine when pasteurised |
| Cooking amounts only | Fine in cooking amounts only |
| Fine as stated | Fine, as stated |

## Pregnancy

### Avoid

- **Chicken liver and beef liver.** Liver is high in vitamin A, which can harm your baby. (<a href="https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/keeping-well/foods-to-avoid/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">NHS, foods to avoid in pregnancy</a>)

### Limit

- **Salmon, mackerel and rainbow trout.** Cooked only, and no more than 2 portions of oily fish a week (about 140 g each). Smoked fish should be cooked until steaming hot. (<a href="https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/eat-well/food-types/fish-and-shellfish-nutrition/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">NHS, fish and shellfish</a>)
- **Green tea.** Caffeine: no more than 200 mg a day. A cup of green tea has about 75 mg. (<a href="https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/keeping-well/foods-to-avoid/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">NHS, foods to avoid in pregnancy</a>)
- **Chamomile tea.** No more than 4 cups of herbal tea a day. (<a href="https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/keeping-well/foods-to-avoid/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">NHS, foods to avoid in pregnancy</a>)

### Cook well

- **Eggs and egg whites.** Eat eggs well cooked. (<a href="https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/keeping-well/foods-to-avoid/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">NHS, foods to avoid in pregnancy</a>)
- **Chicken breast, chicken thigh and turkey.** Cook until there's no trace of pink or blood. (<a href="https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/keeping-well/foods-to-avoid/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">NHS, foods to avoid in pregnancy</a>)
- **Lean beef, goat meat and lamb.** Cook mince, burgers and kebabs until there's no pink or blood. Whole cuts need cooking through on the outside. (<a href="https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/keeping-well/foods-to-avoid/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">NHS, foods to avoid in pregnancy</a>)
- **Anchovies, tilapia and rohu.** Cooked only, never raw. (<a href="https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/keeping-well/foods-to-avoid/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">NHS, foods to avoid in pregnancy</a>)
- **Shrimp, oysters, mussels and clams.** Cooked only. Raw shellfish can make you seriously unwell. (<a href="https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/keeping-well/foods-to-avoid/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">NHS, foods to avoid in pregnancy</a>)

### Pasteurised only

- **Cow's milk, buffalo milk, plain yogurt and Greek yogurt.** Pasteurised only. (<a href="https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/keeping-well/foods-to-avoid/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">NHS, foods to avoid in pregnancy</a>)
- **Cottage cheese, ricotta and paneer.** Fine when made from pasteurised milk. (<a href="https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/keeping-well/foods-to-avoid/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">NHS, foods to avoid in pregnancy</a>)

### Cooking amounts only

- **Fenugreek seeds.** Believed safe in the amounts found in food. Not safe in pregnancy in larger amounts. (<a href="https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/fenugreek" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">NIH NCCIH, fenugreek</a>)

### Fine as stated

- **Cheddar cheese and parmesan.** Hard cheeses are fine, pasteurised or not. (<a href="https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/keeping-well/foods-to-avoid/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">NHS, foods to avoid in pregnancy</a>)
- **Peanuts.** Peanuts and other nuts are fine unless you're allergic or have been told not to have them. (<a href="https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/keeping-well/foods-to-avoid/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">NHS, foods to avoid in pregnancy</a>)

## Trying for a baby

### Limit

- **Salmon, mackerel and rainbow trout.** No more than 2 portions of oily fish a week. (<a href="https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/eat-well/food-types/fish-and-shellfish-nutrition/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">NHS, fish and shellfish</a>)

## Breastfeeding

### Limit

- **Salmon, mackerel and rainbow trout.** No more than 2 portions of oily fish a week. (<a href="https://www.nhs.uk/baby/breastfeeding-and-bottle-feeding/breastfeeding-and-lifestyle/diet/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">NHS, breastfeeding and diet</a>)
- **Green tea.** Caffeine: no more than 200 mg a day. Cutting down matters most while your baby is under 6 months. A mug of green tea has about 38 mg. (<a href="https://www.nhs.uk/baby/breastfeeding-and-bottle-feeding/breastfeeding-and-lifestyle/diet/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">NHS, breastfeeding and diet</a>)

### Cooking amounts only

- **Fenugreek seeds.** Fine in cooking. Little is known about larger amounts while breastfeeding. (<a href="https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/fenugreek" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">NIH NCCIH, fenugreek</a>)

A suggestion, not a diagnosis. Check with your doctor or midwife.
