# How we checked this

Every line in Mother & baby was checked against its source on **11 November 2026**. NHS warning signs are
paraphrased, and UK phone numbers are replaced by your local emergency number. Folate is in µg DFE. Daily needs are
the National Academies RDA and AI for ages 19–30.

## The calls we made

- Feeling sick or being sick: its warning signs are shown as urgent, contact your midwife or doctor today. That's a step up from the NHS, which lists them as non-urgent.
- A high temperature and itchy skin in pregnancy are shown as urgent.
- Fenugreek leaves (methi) get no food-safety row, because a vegetable portion is already a food amount. Fenugreek seeds do have one.
- Guava and orange were taken off the low iron list, because the NHS line they rested on is about iron tablets.
- The emergency line on low mood comes from the NHS page on urgent help for mental health.
- Trimesters follow the NHS week-by-week guide: weeks 1–12, 13–27, and 28 on.
