# Mother & baby

Three stages, each with its own daily needs, foods to take care with, and feelings and conditions:

- **Trying for a baby**
- **Pregnancy**, by trimester and by week, 1 to 42
- **Breastfeeding**

Open it in the app: <a href="https://eatthis.aoneahsan.com/mother" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Mother & baby in Eat This</a>.

## It suggests, it never diagnoses

Every page names foods and gives the reason. None of it is medical advice, a dose or a plan. A suggestion, not a
diagnosis. Check with your doctor or midwife.

Folic acid and vitamin D come up in pregnancy, but Eat This never gives an amount. Ask your doctor, midwife or
pharmacist which one is right for you.

## Warning signs come first

Every feeling and condition starts with its warning signs. If one matches, you get who to contact, not a food list.
The levels are the same as the rest of the app: [Evidence and safety](../evidence-and-safety.md).

## Your stage stays on your phone

Picking a stage only changes what you see: the foods, the limits and the feelings for that stage. The choice is kept
on your phone and never sent anywhere, so Eat This holds no health data about you.
