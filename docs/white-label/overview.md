# White-label and licensing

A business can have Eat This two ways.

- **A white-label licence.** We host and deploy Eat This on your domain, with your name, logo and colours. You get
  six months of updates. You don't get the source code, and we may license Eat This to other businesses too.
- **A buyout.** You get the source of every repository that builds Eat This, six months of updates, and we stop
  licensing it to anyone new. You then pay us 7% of the gross revenue Eat This earns, before sales tax or VAT, every
  quarter, for as long as it earns.

The food catalogue doesn't change under your brand. Every value keeps its USDA FoodData Central or IFCT 2017 record,
and every suggestion keeps the doctor line.

Prices are on the [white-label pricing page](https://eatthis.aoneahsan.com/white-label/pricing). It reads them live
from our licensing service, so this guide doesn't repeat them.
