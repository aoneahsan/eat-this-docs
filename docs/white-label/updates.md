# Updates and renewals

An update is anything we ship for Eat This itself: new foods, corrected values when a source changes, fixes, and new
app releases. Your copy gets them for six months from the day you buy.

After that you can renew for 3, 6 or 12 months on the
[updates page](https://eatthis.aoneahsan.com/white-label/updates), with your licence key and the email on the licence.
A renewal counts from the later of today and the day your current updates end, so renewing early loses nothing.

Without a renewal your copy keeps running on the version it has. A buyer can renew too.
