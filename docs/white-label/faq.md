# White-label questions

**Do I get the code with a licence?**
No. A licence covers us hosting and running Eat This under your brand. The code changes hands only with a buyout.

**Can other businesses license it too?**
Yes, until someone buys it outright. After a buyout nobody new can license it, and licences sold before it keep their
terms.

**Is there a discount for more than one platform group?**
There is when a product offers more than one group. Eat This offers one today, so there's nothing to discount. The
[pricing page](https://eatthis.aoneahsan.com/white-label/pricing) shows the live total.

**Can I add my own foods?**
Yes, as [custom work](https://eatthis.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work). Each food you add needs its own source
record, like every food already in Eat This.

**What happens when my updates end?**
Your copy keeps running on the version it has. [Renew](./updates.md) to keep getting new foods and fixes.

**How do I pay?**
By bank transfer to our Wise account. Start on the [pricing page](https://eatthis.aoneahsan.com/white-label/pricing):
it opens a payment page with our account details and your reference. Once we've confirmed the transfer, your licence
key and certificate come by email.
