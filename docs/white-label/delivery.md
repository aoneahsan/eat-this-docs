# What a licence covers

## The platform group

A licence is priced per platform group. Eat This offers the groups it ships, and the
[pricing page](https://eatthis.aoneahsan.com/white-label/pricing) lists exactly which ones are on sale today.

**Mobile** is the Android app and the iOS app together. We deliver iOS once you provide or fund an Apple developer
account, because an iOS app has to be published from one.

## What we run for you

- Hosting and deployment of your copy
- Your domain connected
- Your name, logo and colours across the app
- A licence key, and a licence file on your site that anyone can check
- Six months of updates from the day you buy

## What a licence leaves out

- **The source code.** It changes hands only with a buyout.
- **Changes only for you**, such as your own foods or a new screen. That's
  [custom work](https://eatthis.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work), quoted on its own.
- **Your own accounts and costs**: your domain, a store developer account, and any paid service you choose.
