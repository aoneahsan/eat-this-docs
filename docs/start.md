# Start here

Eat This answers one question fast: **what natural food should I eat for this, and how much?**

Open [eatthis.aoneahsan.com](https://eatthis.aoneahsan.com). You don't need an account.

## Set these once

1. **Your diet.** Everything, vegetarian, eggetarian, vegan or pescatarian. Every list follows it.
2. **Foods to leave out.** Dairy, eggs, gluten, nuts, peanuts, soy, sesame, fish or shellfish.
3. **Price.** "Keep it cheap" or "best regardless".

All three live under **Settings**, or on the welcome screen the first time you open the app.

## Then pick a way in

- **Now:** good foods for this hour.
- **I need…:** one of ten nutrients.
- **I'm feeling…:** one of ten everyday feelings.
- **Search:** any food, in English or its local name.

Every answer ends with the same line, and we mean it: *a suggestion, not a diagnosis. Check with your doctor,
especially if you're pregnant, on medication or managing a condition.*
