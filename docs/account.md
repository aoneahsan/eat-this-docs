# Your account

You don't need an account to read anything. Signing in with Google keeps your favourites, settings and reminder times on
every device and gives you 10 AI questions a day instead of 3.

## Signing in

Press **Continue with Google** on the [sign-in page](https://eatthis.aoneahsan.com/sign-in). In the Android app, your
phone's own Google account sheet opens instead. The first time you sign in, what you had saved on that device goes up
to your account; after that, each device follows the account.

## Invite friends

Your account page has an invite link. Every 3 friends who sign up with it and confirm their email earn you a month of
Plus, up to 6 months a year. A month earned this way is added on top of any plan you already have.

## Family

On Family, the person who pays invites the others by email from **Account → Family**. Each person signs in with Google
using that email address and keeps their own diet, reminders and favourites. The owner sees who has joined, never
anyone's settings.

## Deleting your account

**Settings → Delete account** removes your profile, favourites, reminders and usage straight away. A paid plan stops
renewing. What's saved on your phone stays until you clear it with **Clear everything on this device**.
