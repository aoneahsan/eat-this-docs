# Your account

You don't need an account to read anything. Signing in with Google keeps your favourites, settings and reminder times on
every device and gives you 10 AI questions a day instead of 3.

## Signing in

Press **Continue with Google** on the [sign-in page](https://eatthis.aoneahsan.com/sign-in). In the Android app, your
phone's own Google account sheet opens instead. The first time you sign in, what you had saved on that device goes up
to your account; after that, each device follows the account.

## Two-step sign-in and other apps

Under **Settings → Security** you can add a code from your phone to every sign-in: see
[Two-step sign-in](./developers/two-step-sign-in.md). Under **Settings → Other apps** you can let a script, Claude or
ChatGPT, your own server or your calendar use your favourites and reminder times: see
[Eat This for developers](./developers/index.md).

## Invite friends

Your account page has an invite link. Every 3 friends who sign up with it and confirm their email earn you a month of
Plus, up to 6 months a year. A month earned this way is added on top of any plan you already have.

## Family

On Family, the person who pays invites the others by email from **Account → Family**. Each person signs in with Google
using that email address and keeps their own diet, [reminder times](./reminders.md) and favourites. The owner sees who has joined, never
anyone's settings.

## Deleting your account

Open [Delete your account](https://eatthis.aoneahsan.com/delete-account), sign in with Google if you aren't already,
type **delete** and press **Delete my account for good**. Your profile, favourites, reminders, usage, invite rewards and
Family place go straight away, and a paid plan ends with the account. In the Android app, tap the person icon at the top,
then **Delete my account**. Payment records Polar must keep by law stay with Polar, no longer linked to you. What's saved
on your phone stays until you clear it with **Clear everything on this device**. Can't get into the Google account? Write
to aoneahsan@gmail.com with the address the account used; it is deleted within 30 days.
