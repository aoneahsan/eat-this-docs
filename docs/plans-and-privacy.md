# Plans and privacy

## Free, Plus and Family

Everything to read is free: every food, list, comparison and warning sign, signed in or not.

| | Free | Plus | Family |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | free | $9.99 a month | $8.99 per person a month, from 3 people |
| Questions a day | 3 as a guest, 10 signed in | 50 | 60 each |
| Reminder times | 3 | 8 | 8 each |
| Daily plan | no | yes | yes |

Every 3 friends who sign up with your link and confirm their email earn you a month of Plus, up to 6 months a year.
Payment is by card through Polar. All payments are final; see the [Terms](https://eatthis.aoneahsan.com/terms).

## What we keep

- Without an account, everything stays on your device.
- With an account (Google sign-in): your name and email, your diet and filters, favourites, reminder times, your plan,
  and how many questions you've asked today.
- **Never:** your exact location, health conditions, symptoms, or the text of your questions.

Full details: [Privacy policy](https://eatthis.aoneahsan.com/privacy).
