How we rank foods

Per serving, never per 100 g​

Nobody eats 100 g of chia seeds. We rank by one ordinary serving: one egg, a handful of peanuts, a cup of cooked daal. Each serving weight comes from the same dataset as the food's nutrients.

A 5% floor​

A food counts as a source only if one serving gives at least 5% of what you need in a day. For omega-3 the floor is 0.1 g of plant ALA, or 0.05 g of fish EPA plus DHA. Below that, a food isn't listed as a source, however healthy it is.

The three rows​

Every nutrient list opens the same way:

RowWhat it is
Bestthe most in one serving, of anything that fits your filters
Best cheapthe most from a food in the cheap price band (it can be the same food as Best)
Worth paying more forthe most from a premium food, never the same as the other two, with one line on what the extra buys

Price bands are for Pakistan (cheap, moderate or premium), checked on 9 November 2026.

Spices, herbs, drinks and honey​

They have their own pages, but they aren't ranked: the amounts people actually use are too small to count.
