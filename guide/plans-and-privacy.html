Plans and privacy

Free, Plus and Family​

Everything to read is free: every food, list, comparison and warning sign, signed in or not.

Every 3 friends who sign up with your link and confirm their email earn you a month of Plus, up to 6 months a year. Payment is by card on Polar's own checkout page. See the Terms for how plans renew and end.

What we keep​

  • Without an account, everything stays on your device.
  • With an account (Google sign-in): your name and email, your diet and filters, favourites, reminder times, your plan, and how many questions you've asked today.
  • Never: your exact location, health conditions, symptoms, or the text of your questions. The Mother & baby stage you pick stays on your phone.
  • Speak: a spoken question is kept by our file service only while it's turned into words, then deleted.
  • Counting and errors: Google Analytics counts page visits (the address of each page, including search words); Microsoft Clarity records how signed-out visitors use public pages; Sentry gets the kind of error and where it happened, never what you typed.
