Connect Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code or Cursor

Add one address to your assistant, sign in to Eat This when it asks, and tick what it may do. Then you can ask it things like "save paneer to my favourites" or "move my lunch reminder to 1:30", and it does it in your Eat This. The address:

https://mbznqopsynqehmpddwbr.supabase.co/functions/v1/mcp

It is the same for everyone; signing in is what ties it to your account. The steps are also under Settings → Other apps → Connect an assistant.

Claude​

  1. In Claude, open Settings, then Connectors, and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Call it Eat This and paste the address above.
  3. Press Connect. Eat This opens; tick what Claude may do and press Approve.

ChatGPT​

  1. In ChatGPT, open Settings, then Apps & Connectors, then Advanced, and turn on Developer mode.
  2. Choose Create, call it Eat This, paste the address and pick OAuth.
  3. Press Create. Sign in to Eat This when it asks, tick what ChatGPT may do and approve.

Claude Code​

  1. In a terminal, run:

    claude mcp add --transport http eat-this https://mbznqopsynqehmpddwbr.supabase.co/functions/v1/mcp

  2. Start Claude Code, type /mcp, pick eat-this and choose Authenticate.

  3. Your browser opens Eat This. Tick what it may do and approve.

Cursor​

  1. Open Cursor Settings, then MCP, and choose Add new MCP server.

  2. Add eat-this under mcpServers:

    {
      "mcpServers": {
        "eat-this": { "url": "https://mbznqopsynqehmpddwbr.supabase.co/functions/v1/mcp" }
      }
    }

  3. Cursor shows Needs login next to eat-this. Press it and approve in Eat This.

What you approve​

Eat This's own page shows which app is asking and where it will send you back. Reading is ticked for you; changing and removing are up to you, from the same list as access keys. Your Family stays read only. Your plan, payment, email and two-step sign-in are never reachable. You can give the connection your own name under Call this connection, and the page shows how many connections your plan has left (3 on Free, 10 on Plus, 25 on Family). We email you when an app connects.

The assistant sees only the tools your ticks allow, plus the public food catalogue. Each record type has list_ and get_ tools, such as list_favourites or get_reminders; where you allowed changes there are create_favourites, create_reminders, update_reminders and update_preferences; where you allowed removing there are prepare_delete_favourites and prepare_delete_reminders. A list returns up to 50 items with a cursor for more.

Removing things waits for you​

An assistant never removes anything by itself. When it asks to remove a saved food or a reminder time, Eat This makes a link, eatthis.aoneahsan.com/developers/confirm/…, which the assistant hands to you. The request also shows under Settings → Other apps → Connected apps → Waiting for you.

The page shows what would go: one saved food (its page stays, and you can save it again), or one reminder time (your phone stops reminding you at that time; the others stay). Nothing happens unless you press Remove. Keep it leaves everything as it is, and the request runs out by itself 10 minutes after the assistant asked. A request that ran out removes nothing.

The same holds for any app you connected through Eat This's sign-in page and that uses the API: its removal answers 202 with a confirm_url and an expires_at instead of removing.

Disconnecting​

Open Settings → Other apps → Connected apps and press Remove next to the assistant. It can't reach your Eat This from that moment, even with a token it already holds. Rename changes the name you see there.

Assistant requests count towards your plan's requests a day, with your access keys.
